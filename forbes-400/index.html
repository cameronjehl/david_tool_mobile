How to read this

  • Source. Forbes' 2026 Forbes 400 list, retrieved from Forbes' list API on 15 September 2026. Net worths are Forbes' figures as of the list's publication.
  • Fields. The 17 industry categories are Forbes' own, one per member. Percentages are simple head counts out of 400.
  • Finance sub-fields. Forbes puts every financier in one category. The split into private equity, hedge funds, trading, asset management, venture capital, banking, financial data, crypto and holding companies was assigned by hand from each member's primary vehicle. "Private equity & buyouts" is where the deal-making and M&A fortunes sit: KKR, Blackstone, Apollo, Carlyle, TPG, Thoma Bravo, Vista, Clearlake, H.I.G. and their peers.
  • Wealth share. A field's combined net worth divided by the list's total. Technology's share is dominated by a handful of fortunes at the top of the list, so read it as concentration, not a typical outcome.
